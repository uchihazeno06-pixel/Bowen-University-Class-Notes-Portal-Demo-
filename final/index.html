Add a Note

Notes you add are public: every department can see them. Deleting a note only hides it for your department. You can add notes to courses related to your department, including General courses.

Request a Course for Your Department

Want another department's course to show under your department? Send a request. The rep of that course's department (or the admin) must approve it.
